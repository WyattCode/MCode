MCode Remote: every question, and checkbox questions behave like checkboxes

The question tool sends a list - a model may put three things to the reader at
once - and the card rendered `questions[0]` and dropped the rest. A request for
two answers could only ever be given one, and the server waited for an answer
that never came. `multiple` was ignored for the same reason: a checkbox
question behaved like a radio one, so a reader could pick a single item from a
list that allowed several.

All questions are drawn now, in order, and a `multiple` question accumulates
while a single-answer one replaces the choice. The answers are gathered in
question order on 发送; with one question and one answer the tap still replies
immediately, which is the common case and should stay one tap.

Build: gradle assembleDebug, installed on the pad (M367FC).
