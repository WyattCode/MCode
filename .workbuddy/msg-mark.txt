MCode Remote: the empty pane's mark, sized to what it draws

The asterisk's ink spans 103px on the pad - 32.6dp - and a 32.6dp box drew
only 85px of it. The gap is the drawing rather than the measurement: the twelve
spokes are stroked lines that stop at the radius, so the round cap keeps half a
stroke inside the box, and no spoke points along an axis.

32.6 x 103/85 = 39.5, which draws 33.0dp of ink and lands its centre at 2195
against the pane's 2199. An earlier pass had already corrected 40 to 32.6 by
measuring the ink and trusting the box; this corrects the box to what the ink
needs.

Worth stating plainly because it is the third time this shape has fooled me:
when a drawn mark measures small, the number to change is the box, not the
drawing. Measuring the ink and then setting that same number as the size is a
loop that never closes.
