MCode Remote: the user bubble, measured in Claude's own transcript

Got a bubble to measure at last: sent one short message in Claude's chat pane
and read its rect back.

    bubble        94 x 120px = 29.8 x 38.0dp
    corner        20 (edge leaves the top at 22px in)
    inset         9.2 / 9.2 / 10.8 / 10.5 - 38dp around a 21dp line
    from the
    right edge    90.3dp
    the reply     no bubble at all, plain text from 1274px in, with a six-mark
                  action row under it (copy, share, read, up, down, retry)

Three things were wrong, and the first is the one a reader would feel:

  - The bubble was `fillMaxWidth(0.78f)`. Against the reference's 29.8dp for
    「Hi」 that is a slab three quarters of the pane wide for every turn,
    whatever was in it - a two-word question drawn as a paragraph, in the
    reader's own voice. It hugs its words now, capped at 520dp.

  - The corner was 16 and the inset 13/9; measured, it is 20 and 9.2/10.8. The
    old pair was a tighter pill, which made a short question read as a chip.

  - It sat 13dp from the pane's edge, where the reference stops 90.3dp out -
    clear of the header's trailing icons. A long prompt ran under them.

One thing that was not a bubble at all: an unnamed conversation showed 「MCode」
in the header, because `currentSessionTitle` fell back to the app's own name.
That reads as a title that failed to load, and the reference simply has no title
line until the first words name it. It returns "" now.

Not yet matched: the six-mark action row under each reply. The phone has copy
and branch on a long press; the reference draws the row inline, always visible,
100px marks with 10 between them.
