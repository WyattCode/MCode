MCode Remote: no drag handle between the panes

The split had a draggable handle: a 32dp grab target with a 4dp bar inside it,
sitting in the seam between the list and the transcript. It came from Claude's
Code screen, which genuinely has one - that screen trades width between a device
list and a session. Its chat screen does not: the panes share the page colour
and nothing is drawn in the seam.

Two things followed from it. The chrome had one element that looked like a
control in a page that otherwise has no edges - visible at any glance as a small
bar floating between two columns. And because `draggable` claims the hit test,
it sat in the middle of the seam waiting to swallow a gesture.

`ColumnDivider` is deleted rather than left unused: its whole body was the
handle, and a dead composable that draws this is exactly what a later pass would
call back into.

Left alone, and worth writing down because it looks related: a 7dp band of
horizontal stripes at x=960..982, y=1039..1113. It is not this handle - the
handle is a solid rounded bar, 4 x 48 - and it is not a Composited scrollbar,
which LazyColumn does not draw. Its colours (185 on 249) appear nowhere in the
source. It sits in the seam at the exact x where the drawer's content is
clipped, which points at the ModalNavigationDrawer shell rather than at
anything this file draws. Unresolved.
