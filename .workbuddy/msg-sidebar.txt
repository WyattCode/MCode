MCode Remote: the conversation list, measured off the app

Same method as the composer, and it turned up the same class of error: every
face in the list was a size small, and two pieces of the screen were not there
at all.

Measured off Claude's own list on this pad (3408x2272, 3.156 px per dp):

  sidebar          900px = 285dp
  search field     840 x 120px = 266 x 38dp, corner so round it is a capsule
  conversation row 153px = 48.5dp   (was 138)
  title glyphs     60px = 19dp      -> 16sp   (was 14)
  timestamp        53px = 17dp      -> 14sp   (was 12)
  top bar          242px = 77dp

The two missing pieces:

  - The search field is always on in Claude's list, under the title. The phone
    hid it behind a magnifier, so reaching it was two gestures and the list
    looked emptier than the reference. The magnifier is gone and the field is
    always drawn; it takes no focus until tapped, so the keyboard does not
    open on every visit.

  - The open conversation had a grey slab behind it. Claude has no such fill -
    its own open row is the page's colour edge to edge, marked by a dot at the
    row's trailing end. Ours read as a menu with a selected item.

One thing that broke and had to be undone: fixing the field to a 38dp height
clipped its placeholder into an ellipsis, because Material's field needs 56 for
its own padding at a 15sp face. It is a minimum now, not a height.

Still off, and visible side by side: our rows start at 101px against the
reference's 120 (a 6dp icon indent), and the row icons are a plain rounded
square where Claude draws a speech bubble or a code mark.
