MCode Remote: a leading slash reaches the commands

The phone had no way to type a slash command. `/init`, `/review`, `/dream`,
`/distill`, `/rebuild` and the rest exist on the desktop and in every folder
that defines them; on the phone they were simply unreachable, and a prompt that
began with a slash went to the model as literal text instead.

The composer now asks `command.list` for the session's folder, opens a filtered
menu when the box begins with `/`, and completes the name on a tap so arguments
can follow. The menu floats above the card - the desktop's opens upward over the
conversation - because growing it inside the card pushed the field, and the
thing being typed, down away from the finger.

Build: gradle assembleDebug, installed on the pad (M367FC); typing `/` listed
all six commands of this repository with their descriptions.
