MCode Remote: 正在重新连接, with its attempt and reason

`RetryRow.vue`: while the provider request is being tried again the transcript
says so - a reconnect mark, the attempt count, and the failure's own words
after a middot - rather than sitting on a silent, frozen turn. The phone had
nothing here, so a retry looked exactly like the app having hung.

`retry` rides only on the copy published with `message.updated` and is never
written to disk, which decides two things: the field is assigned rather than
merged (a stale attempt would otherwise outlive the retry it described), and a
message whose only content is that retry still has to become a row - otherwise
the update that says "trying again" draws nothing at all.

The row itself is unverified on the pad: it needs a provider that fails and
retries, which this machine's key does not produce on demand. The shape it
reads is confirmed against `src/shared/protocol.ts`.
