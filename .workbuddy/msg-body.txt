MCode Remote: the transcript's leading, and the list's glyphs

Measured against Claude's own transcript on this pad, the body text was set a
step too large and spaced ten times too far apart:

  line of body   64px = 20.3dp  -> 15sp on a 20sp line   (was 17/26)
  gap between
  paragraphs     10px = 3.2dp   -> 3.2dp                 (was 14)

The gap was the bigger half of it. Two sentences of one paragraph stood 40dp
apart here and 3 on the reference, so every reply read as loosely set even
where its words were identical. With both corrected a paragraph of the same
words occupies the same share of the pane in both apps.

The conversation glyphs were being drawn as empty boxes. `M5 5h14v14H5z` is a
closed rectangle, and `MiIcon` strokes rather than fills, so a closed path
becomes its own outline - which is what every row showed. They are an outline
`<>` for a conversation working in a folder and an outline speech bubble for
one that is not, matching Claude's own split.

One correction to the record: Claude's app on this pad opens onto its Code
remote-control screen, not a chat transcript, so the bubble geometry below the
fold cannot be sampled from it. The two numbers above came from its rendered
text nodes (`uiautomator`), which are the real thing; the rest of the
transcript - bubble radii, the user turn's fill, the action row - is still on
the desktop stylesheet and possibly a different design at this size, exactly as
the composer was.
