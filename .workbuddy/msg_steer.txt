MCode Remote: a mid-turn message says it was heard

Sending during a running turn delivers the words into that turn (the server
marks the message `steered`), and the desktop answers with a note under the
bubble: 「已插话 · 会尽快处理」. The phone showed the bubble and nothing else, so
a message typed mid-reply looked like it might have been swallowed - and there
was no way to tell whether the model had it.

The message now carries the server's own `steered` flag through both the live
event and the stored transcript, and the note sits under the bubble in the same
words the desktop uses.

Build: gradle assembleDebug, installed on the pad (M367FC); the note appears
under the steered bubble.
