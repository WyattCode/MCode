MCode Remote: a sent picture is a picture, not an icon

Claude draws a sent image at about 235px on the pad - 84dp at 400dpi - with
12dp corners. This drew it at 64dp, which on a tablet reads as a clip rather
than as the shot that was sent; the whole point of showing it above the bubble
is that a picture can be recognised without opening it.

Build: gradle assembleDebug, installed on the pad (M367FC).
