MCode Remote: the projects header draws all four of its parts

The header showed its burger and nothing else - no title, no magnifier, no
filter - and card rows began at y 152 where the reference begins them at 282.

Found by painting the header red: the Box measured its full 360 by 63.2 at
y 32.8..95.6, exactly right, and inside it only the burger's ink appeared. Three
faults stacked:

1. `if (leftMode == LeftMode.Projects)` near the top of App had no width guard,
   so on the pad it painted a full-screen copy of the page - with its own
   status-bar spacer, which pushed everything 32.8 down - and then the wide
   branch painted the real page over the top. The copy underneath is what ate the
   space. It is guarded to Compact now, the way 设置 already was.

2. The title was `fillMaxSize().wrapContentHeight()`, which in a fixed-height Box
   measures to no height at all. Same fault as the home header two passes ago,
   in three more places; all four state their height now.

3. `horizontalArrangement = SpaceBetween` spread the burger and the two marks
   evenly across the bar, which put the magnifier on top of the title's last
   character - the build read 「项(放大镜)目」. A weight before the marks keeps them
   at the trailing edge, where the reference has them.

Measured after: 项目 runs x 168.8..196.0 against the reference's 164.0..194.0,
magnifier and filter at the right, burger at x 15.6..31.6.
