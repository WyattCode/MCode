MCode Remote: the home title, and the scheduled page's empty block

The home title was 18sp and ran 70.8 wide where the reference's runs 65.2 - the
same four characters, so 16.6sp. Its centre was 5 left of the column's 180.0, and
the -5.2 offset that was supposed to fix that had the sign backwards: +5.0 pushed
it to 185.6 and the right number is -0.8. Verified: 147.2..212.0 at 64.8 across,
centre 179.6, against 147.2..212.4 at 65.2, centre 179.8.

The scheduled page's empty block sat 206 above its line. Three attempts at the
size modifier - fillMaxWidth, fillMaxHeight, fillMaxSize - left the block at the
identical y 243.2, so whatever constrains that column is upstream of it and not
something I was going to find by changing its own modifier again. The block is
placed by a stated top pad instead: 327.8, from the reference's disc topping at
429.4 with the header ending at 95.6. Verified: 449.6, 498.8, 528.8 against
449.6, 497.2, 526.4.

Two sizes inside the block were also wrong and are now measured rather than
guessed. The clock's ink ran 14.8 in a 20 dp box; the reference's is 23.6, so the
box is 31.9 - a stroked ring stops at its radius, so the box has to be larger than
the mark. And the spacer under the disc was 14 where the reference leaves 3.8
between the disc's foot and the title's ink; it is 0 now, with the disc's own
64 dp carrying the rest.

arrow in the home header. There is none. Scanned across, the bar holds the burger
at 15.6..31.6, the title, the filter at 278.8..292.0 and the list mark at
