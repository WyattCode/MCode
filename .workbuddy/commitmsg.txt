fix(android): a tool row's three greys, and its mark at the right size

The row was all one colour and its icon was a third too small, so a list of
calls read as a flat wall of grey with a dot in front.

Sampled the reference's own expanded rows and it uses three weights on one
line: the mark at (56,56,53), the verb 「已运行」 at (19,19,19) - the body
text colour - and what the call acted on at (123,121,116). The summary line
above them is darker than the muted grey too, at (83,82,79) against this
app's `InkMuted` of (107,104,99).

Now measured on the pad, on the same four points:

  summary     (83,82,79)   reference (83,82,79)
  mark        (56,56,53)   reference (56,56,53)
  verb        (31,30,28)   reference (19,19,19)
  target      (123,121,116) reference (123,121,116)

The verb lands on this app's ink rather than the reference's literal 19
because those are the same weight in two themes; the smallest pixel in an
antialiased CJK glyph differs by a few levels either way.

The mark grows from a 14 box with a 6 gap to 15.6 with 16, which puts its
ink at 11.6 high - the reference's own measurement - against the 10.4 it
drew before. Its width still reads narrower, 11.6 against 15.6: that is the
glyph, a round magnifier here and a wide fork for a shell call there, and
each is the right mark for its own tool family.

The verb keeps carrying the state colour when a call failed or is still
running, since that is the part a reader scans for.
