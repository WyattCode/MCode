MCode Remote: the filter and list marks, drawn from 5x crops

Both of the home header's trailing marks were wrong shapes, and neither is
readable at thumbnail scale.

The filter is not a funnel. At 5x it is three full-height rules, each carrying a
hollow ring, the rings stepping high, low, middle left to right, with the rule
stopping at each rim rather than running through. Drawing one continuous rule and
an arc over it made a figure-eight at every ring - the mark read as three
mysterious beads. The rules are split around the circles now, in the reference's
own #383835 rather than the muted grey inherited from the clock this replaced:
measured, its ink is (56,56,53), 16.0 x 13.6.

The list mark is ticks beside rules, not bullets. Enlarged, each row opens with a
three-stroke tick.

Composer width. The reference's card is 766.4 inside a 1003.2 pane - 130.4 left,
106.8 right - and this filled the pane minus 12 of padding, so the card ran 951.6
with its right edge 14.4 from the seam. The inset goes on the card's own Surface,
which is where the fillMaxWidth actually was; a first attempt put it on a Column
two hundred lines above and changed nothing.
