MCode Remote: the scheduled page's height, narrowed to the size modifier

A marker proved the embedded branch runs: drawn in magenta, it appeared at
y 51.2 with 1460 lit pixels. So the empty block's 200 dp of drift is not a wrong
render path, and my earlier conclusion - that the branch never ran - was wrong;
the green wash I tried first had simply been painted over by the page's own
header.

With the path confirmed, the fault is the size modifier. This had been reduced to
`fillMaxWidth()` when embedded, which leaves the column with no height constraint:
the caller's `weight(1f)` allocates the space, the column then measures to its
children, and the empty state's own `weight(1f)` finds nothing left to divide. Its
block centred at 486 on a page whose ink runs to 880 - it believed the page was
972 tall.

`fillMaxHeight()` asks for the height the weight already granted without claiming
a second one. Built clean; the verifying screenshot did not happen, because adb
dropped this pad four times in a row and the daemon restarted underneath it.

Also seen while trying to reach the page: the home screen now carries a trailing
back arrow it should not have, and the drawer's burger did not open the drawer on
three taps at two coordinates. Both need checking once the link is stable - the
arrow and the burger may be the same fault.
