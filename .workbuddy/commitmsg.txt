Two numbers I had recorded were wrong, and both came from scanning in the wrong
place. The well is 112 x 74.8, not 155.2 x 86.4 - a vertical scan at x = 80 dp puts
its fill from 192.8 to 267.6 and a horizontal one at y = 224 puts its edges at 28.0
and 140.0. And the card's inset is 12, from 16.0 to 28.0 and from 180.8 to 192.8,
not 8. Between them those two errors drew a card 14.4 taller than the reference's.

The colours were the bigger fault. This filled the card with `surface`
(254,254,254), which made every record read as a white tile floating over a grey
page; the reference's card is the page colour exactly, and the only thing darker
on it is the well. Sampled at x 200 y 224 inside the reference's card: (249,249,247).

The three text lines are placed off their own ink positions - 24.0 then 30.4 apart,
the title 4.8 under the well's top - rather than stacked with equal spacers, which
had put them at 34.4 and 36.0.
