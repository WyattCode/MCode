feat(android): a patch card says how much it is not showing

`DiffLines` drew at most 80 lines and dropped the rest without a word. Across the
47 patches in this machine's transcripts the median is 15 lines - but the longest
runs to 99, so a reader could reach the bottom of a card with a third of the
change behind the fold and no way to tell.

It now ends with 「…还有 19 行」 when there is more. The count is the one thing a
reader cannot infer from what is on screen.

The arithmetic lives in `diffSlice(diff, maxLines) -> (shown, hidden)`, split out
of the composable so it can be tested without a device, and it reads only
`maxLines + 1` lines to decide: the first version of this fix materialised the
whole patch to count it, which a pathological diff would have paid for.

Nine tests hold it (`DiffSliceTest`), 100 in the suite:

- a short patch is drawn whole, and 80 of 80 is not a cut either;
- one line past the limit reports exactly one hidden;
- the 99-line maximum on this machine reports 19;
- a patch with no trailing newline still counts its last line;
- order is preserved and a real unified diff keeps its hunk header.

Not done, and recorded as such: the card's *colours* - added `#20A923`, removed
`#E5351C`, `@@` in the muted grey - are still taken from the desktop's diff
component, not measured off the phone. The reference thread that carries a step
turned out to hold no file edits, so there was nothing to sample them from this
pass; guessing at them again would repeat the mistake the rail made.
