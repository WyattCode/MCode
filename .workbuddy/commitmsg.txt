MCode Remote: one hairline token was serving two different lines

The 添加设备 capsule lost its outline when the card hairline was corrected last
pass, because both drew from LocalCardLine. They are not the same colour: the
card edge reads (248,248,246) on a (249,249,247) page - one step, invisible on
purpose - while the capsule edge reads (225,225,223), which you can see. The
token is split: LocalCardHairline for the card, LocalCardLine for anything that
is meant to be a line.

The capsule itself was also wrong in three ways, all measured:

Its content sat at 14 dp inset with 13 sp text and a 6 dp gap after the plus,
against the reference 9.6 inset, 14 sp and 13.2 gap - which is why it came out
101.6 wide where the reference is 73.6.

And it has no fill at all. Sampled at three points inside the reference pill:
(249,249,247), the page colour. I had read it as a white plate off the
thumbnail - the pixels say it is an outline on the page and nothing else.

Pill width, height and fill are now the reference's numbers. The fill change is
the only one of these that was not photographed: the pad locked itself and adb
cannot light it on battery, five attempts in a row.
