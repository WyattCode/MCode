MCode Remote: the list bullet sits in the prose column, not inset from it

Re-read A_claude.png for how a list is set, and the earlier numbers were wrong in
a way that only a stacked strip made visible: I cut the left 30 dp of seven
consecutive lines and joined them into one column, so the glyphs could be read
instead of guessed at.

    prose          408.8
    top-level •    408.8
    nested ○       429.2

So a bullet shares the prose column's left edge - zero inset - and each level
below it steps 20.4.

This had `8.8 + depth x 20.4`, and its comment cites the reading it came from:
"the top-level • starts at 48.8 dp and the nested ○ at 69.2, against a prose edge
of 40.0". Both halves of that were wrong. 48.8 was not where the bullet sat, and
40.0 was the reference's Code pane edge - the conversation pane's is 48.8, which I
corrected earlier this session. The 8.8 inset put every top-level bullet a step
right of the prose it belongs to, and the 20.4 step compounded it, landing a
nested item 29.2 in where the reference has 20.4.
