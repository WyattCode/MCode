MCode Remote: the bullet steps down with the list, it does not repeat

Side by side against the reference, the last visible difference in a list was the
glyph: the reference draws a filled `•` at the top level and a hollow `○` one
level in, and this drew `•` at every depth. A nested item was told apart only by
its indent, which is the weaker of the two signals - the glyph is what says "this
belongs under the line above" without the reader measuring the margin.

    depth 0  •      (reference: solid at 408.8)
    depth 1  ○      (reference: hollow at 429.2)
    depth 2  ▪

Task lists keep their ✓/○, and ordered lists keep their numbers, so the step only
applies to bullets.

Verified on the build: 第一条 and 第二条 carry `•`, 嵌套的一条 carries `○` and
sits 20.4 in, 第三条 back to `•`.
