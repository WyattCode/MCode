MCode Remote: the badge is a palette, not a brush

Side by side with the reference, the only remaining difference on the artifacts
card was the mark at the thumbnail's corner. I had drawn a brush on a circle
overhanging the corner; enlarged 6x, the reference's is a palette - a rounded body
with a notch bitten out at its lower right and four dots on its face - on a 23.6
square that sits flush with the well's left edge and stands on its bottom edge
(x 28.0..51.6, y 243.6..267.2 against a well running to 267.6).

Verified: the sticker now measures x 28.0..51.2, y 242.8..266.0 - 23.2 square
against 23.6 - in #DEDBFA with #5C50AC strokes.

The dots took two passes. The first put the upper row at 0.40 of the height, which
is exactly where the outline runs, so they read as notches cut out of the stroke
rather than marks on the face. At 0.44 with a 1.15 radius they float.
