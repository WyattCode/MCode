feat(android): join the rows of one expanded group with the reference's hairline

Opening a folded group gave a stack of rows with no thread between them, so a
list of calls read as separate entries rather than as one step.

Measured the reference's connector between two of its expanded rows: a 0.8 rule
at x 395.2..396.0 in (216,216,214). Its mark spans 387.6..403.2, so 395.4 is
that mark's own centre - the line is not a column of its own but drops out of
the icon, which is what makes it read as one list.

Drawn in the row's `drawBehind`, at 7.8 from the column's edge (half the 15.6
slot) and from below the mark's ink to the row's bottom, so the next row's line
picks it up without a seam. `linked` is false on the group's last row, and the
line is only drawn for rows that carry `indent` - a lone call has nothing to be
joined to.

On the pad it lands at x 439.2..440.0 in exactly (216,216,214).
