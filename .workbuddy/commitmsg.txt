MCode Remote: a class cast that killed the app on launch, and the rows it hid

The `as Alignment` I added two passes ago to satisfy the compiler was wrong and
fatal: Alignment.Top and Alignment.TopStart are different classes, and the cast
threw ClassCastException on the first frame - so the app crashed on launch and
never got as far as drawing anything. Every screenshot I took afterwards was of
Claude or of the launcher, and I read their pixels as if they were this app's.

The cause was mine: an if/else chain over Alignment was inferred as Any, and
rather than restructure I cast it. The right answer was a `when` whose branches
are all the same type, and Alignment.TopStart does the job Alignment.Top was
wanted for - top and left, which is where the mark hangs.

With the crash gone, the home list measures:

  顶栏      55.2  against 56.4
  搜索框    122.0 against 119.2
  first title 175.2 against 174.0
  title ink 14.0 against 12.8
  time ink  12.4 against 13.6

and the row pitch was the remaining fault: 80.4 between titles where the
reference runs 60.0, because the flat rows were wearing the cards' 68 dp height.
Now 60.0, with the time 3 dp under the title instead of 6.
