MCode Remote: three colours and two sizes, read off the reference side by side

Putting the two columns next to each other at last, at the same scale, showed
four things that no measurement had caught:

The card hairline was #E1E1DF - twenty-four steps below the page. The
reference edge pixels read (248,248,246) on a (249,249,247) page: one step. Its
cards are soft panels whose shape carries the separation; ours were outlined
boxes. That single colour is most of why the two columns read differently even
where every number matched.

The card spans x 12.0..347.6 in the reference and 14.0..345.6 here - two dp
narrow on both sides.

The separator between 已连接 and Remote control was U+00B7, which sits on the
baseline of a Latin face; the reference uses U+2022, a filled dot at mid-height.

The running card's mark is a 20.0 x 14.0 open arc, not the 18 dp hairline hoop
this drew.

All four are changed. Only the ones before this batch were photographed on glass:
the pad sleeps within seconds on battery and adb cannot wake it (KEYCODE_WAKEUP,
am start, swipe, stayon - all report success, panel stays black, eight retries).
So the four above are reference measurements and the build compiles, and that is
the whole of what can honestly be claimed for them right now.
