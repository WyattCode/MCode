MCode Remote: the idle composer teaches the slash, and the prose breathes less

Two measurements off Claude's own screen on the pad:

- its idle placeholder reads 「输入 / 以使用命令」 - it shows the slash commands
  exactly where they would be typed - in 123,121,116, a step lighter than the
  muted used for labels. Ours said 「与 MCode 对话…」 in a darker grey, in a
  product that has had slash commands since this week.
- its prose is 53px per line, 19sp at 400dpi. Ours was set to 23sp, which made
  the same paragraph a third taller than the original and every reply longer to
  read than Claude's.

Build: gradle assembleDebug, installed on the pad (M367FC).
