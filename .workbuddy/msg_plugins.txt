MCode Remote: the plugins page, which the phone could not reach at all

PluginsView.vue lists what the model can reach - the MCP servers and the
folder's skills - and the phone had no equivalent, so a skill could not be
switched off and a server that failed to start could not even be seen. It is a
whole page like the artifacts centre, reached from the drawer: 插件 at 24sp, the
desktop's subtitle, 搜索插件和技能, the three status chips (全部 / 已启用 / 已停用),
then 已安装 over the cards. A server card shows 共 N 个工具, or its failure in the
server's own words; a skill card shows its description, or 内置技能 / 技能 when it
has none, with a switch.

The switch writes the field the loader reads: config.get, the one list changed,
config.set on disabledSkills - the same arithmetic PluginsView.vue does. A page
that cannot read either list says 无法读取本机已安装的插件和技能 rather than
showing an empty page, which would read as nothing installed.

Compile-checked only: the pad's keyguard is showing and its password cannot be
entered over adb, so this and the three commits before it are unverified.
