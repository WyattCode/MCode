MCode Remote: the project's files, readable from the phone

The desktop keeps a whole right-hand rail - files, changes, review, subagents,
terminal, version history - and the phone had none of it. A tablet has the room
for the first of them, and it is the shortest way to answer "what is actually
in this folder" without asking the model to list it.

项目文件 in the conversation's overflow opens the folder one level at a time,
with the path as a breadcrumb and a back arrow, directories first and files
after. A file opens in its own sheet as monospaced text; the preview is a
separate sheet because a bottom sheet measures its height on open and clipped
anything added to it later.

Both round trips use the screen's own `rpc`; the hand-rolled ticket that was
tried first never resolved.

Build: gradle assembleDebug, installed on the pad (M367FC); the folder listed
and LICENSE.electron.txt read through in full.
