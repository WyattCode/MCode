MCode Remote: a step's error row, and the notice under an interactive call

Two rows the desktop has and the phone did not, ErrorStep.vue and
NoticeRow.vue - the same row with a different mark: `.hstep`'s 5 above and
below, 14 over a 22.4 line, a 14-wide column, and a plain monospaced ! or i in
it with nothing drawn round either.

The error row matters more than it looks. The phone drew every failure as a
card at the end of the turn, so a turn that hit a length limit, compacted and
carried on still ended on a red card - the reader was told the turn broke when
it had not. Now a failure the turn stepped past settles into 「错误：…」 where it
happened, and only one that ends the turn keeps its card.

Getting that right took three wrong answers, all found by logging what the
list actually held rather than by reasoning about it:

  - The card is not the last row. A wordless failed reply gets a row of its own
    too, so the tail read [Msg, Err, Msg] and a lastOrNull test found the plain
    Msg and converted nothing.
  - It is that wordless row that carries the finish, not a blank one - the tail
    was [Msg(fin=), Msg(fin=max_tokens), Err] - so the row to clear is found by
    its finish, not by its empty text.
  - Clearing it is what removes 「回复已达长度上限，内容被截断」, a sentence that
    is wrong once the turn did not end there.

Verified end to end against a real interactive bash call (the model sends
interactive: true, and the tool answers with metadata.interactive) and against
a session written with a failed-then-continued message pair; both were removed
afterwards. 18 sessions before, 18 after.
