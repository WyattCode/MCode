MCode Remote: the turn's edited files, as one card

The card never appeared before because it was reading the wrong field:
`state.output` carries a tool's one-line reply, while the change lives in
`metadata.diff`. Measured over the eighteen conversations on this machine -
1306 tool calls, 244 with a diff, none with a patch in their output.

Now the patch is parsed the way `src/shared/unified-diff.ts` parses it. Hunks
are read by their own line counts rather than by `+`/`-` prefixes, because a
removed "-- x" is written "--- x" and with a "++ y" after it looks exactly
like the next file's header.

A turn's edits are gathered into one card, as `EditedFilesCard.vue` has them,
and it goes under the row that ends the turn. Two things that took measuring
to find:

  - The row container was a Box, so the card stacked on top of the paragraph
    it was meant to follow; 「起更新了。」 sat behind the card's edge.

  - A gap or a failure could not be allowed to set the turn's end, or the
    card landed after the failure notice and read as if the thing that broke
    had edited the files.

撤销 and 审阅 come with it: the revert asks first in the desktop's orange
card, then reloads the conversation so the files and the card's reverted form
both come from the server. Verified end to end on the pad - a turn that
created `mcode-outside-probe.txt` answered {restored:0, removed:1} and the
card took its 「已撤销 1 个文件的改动」 form. 审阅 opens the turn's own
recorded patch.
