MCode Remote: the gap marker is a rule, not a wave

The divider between sittings was drawn as a real sine - six quadratic humps
across the pane at 1.5dp of stroke. Claude's is the plainest mark in its
transcript: one hairline at the weakest text step, broken in the middle by the
clock.

Seen side by side, the wave was the loudest thing on a page that is otherwise
all restraint, and the only element that made the phone's transcript look drawn
by hand rather than set.

No measurement is behind this one - I could not get the reference to show a
gap marker on demand, since its chat pane only draws one when a conversation
has been left and returned to. The change is from the shape being wrong rather
than from a number being wrong: a sine at 1.5dp is not what any of these
interfaces do, and 1px is what the rest of the page's rules use.
