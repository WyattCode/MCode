MCode Remote: the scheduled page listens instead of assuming

This page said 「MCode 还没有定时任务。桌面端以后提供自动化时，它们会出现在这里。」
as a permanent sentence. That was a guess, and a wrong one: the server has had
scheduled tasks all along and broadcasts them as `automation.tasks` to whoever
is listening.

The event is handled now and the tasks are listed with their title and next run
time; when there are none, the page shows Claude's own empty state - a clock, a
title and one line under it - rather than a sentence claiming the feature does
not exist.

Build: gradle assembleDebug, installed on the pad (M367FC); the page rendered
its empty state with no tasks configured.
