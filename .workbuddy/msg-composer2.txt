MCode Remote: the composer is now the reference's shape, measured twice

The card was 41dp narrower than Claude's - the largest single difference
between the two composers - and the row inside it had three controls where the
reference has two. Measured off Claude's composer on this pad:

  card left edge   1002px  -> 12.7dp   (carried 44)
  card right edge  13.6dp  from the edge (carried 30)
  card width       748.8dp             (was 707.6)
  card bottom      25.4dp above the screen's edge (was 19.0)
  plus, mic, send  22.5dp discs        (were 24, and the send was a 28 tile)
  model pill       28.5 tall           (29 now, was right)

Three things were not a size question:

  - The send disc had a near-black fill whenever the box was empty. Claude's is
    #F0EFEC with a dark mark in every state - so the loudest thing in the card
    was the control that could do the least. It is a rounded-square outline now,
    which is the mark the reference uses, not the waveform this had.

  - The row carried a permission pill and a context ring. Claude's row is a plus
    and exactly one pill, with nothing between; both are one tap away, the
    permission switch at the foot of the plus menu and the context figures on
    the pill itself, so nothing is lost - but the row is the reference's shape.

  - The card's shadow fell in every direction. At 6dp the wash above it ran from
    249 to 241 over six pixels; the reference has nothing above the card and its
    whole drop below. Lowered to 4, and the edge from 1dp to 0.6 - the
    reference's lip is two pad pixels, which is 0.63.

Found on the way: the gateway had died mid-session, and the phone was showing
「连接已断开」 over an empty list that said 「还没有对话」. That line was added last
turn for the case where a filter matches nothing, and on a dropped connection it
claims the machine has no conversations at all. Restarting the gateway brought
all 18 rows back, so the state itself is the reconnect banner's job, not this
line's - but the wording is still wrong for a lost connection and is worth a
third case.
