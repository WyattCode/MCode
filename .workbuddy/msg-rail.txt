MCode Remote: the turn rail, and delivered files under the reply

The rail is `SessionSpine.vue`: a tick per question down the left edge, 9px
away from the pointer and swelling to 24 within three on a Gaussian falloff,
so it reads as one arrow rather than as a bar chart. Ticks in view are drawn
in the strong ink and the rest in the weak one, which is what makes the rail
say where the reader is. A tap anywhere on a tick's row goes to that turn -
a 9px hairline is a hopeless target, so the whole column listens, as the
desktop's full-height rail does.

The desktop waits for three exchanges before showing it; one or two ticks say
nothing a scroll would not. Verified on the pad against a conversation with
many turns: the rail paints, and a tap moves the transcript to that turn and
repaints the strong ticks around it.

Delivered files had the same fault the edited card had. `DeliveredCards` was
painted inside the tool row behind an `onOpenDelivered` argument that only the
bare `is Item.Tool ->` branch passed - and a call almost never reaches that
branch, because `rowsOf` folds neighbours into a `ToolRun` and `stepsOf` folds
those into the `Step` above. A reply that presented a document showed
「展示文件」 and no card. They are now gathered per turn and drawn under the
edited card, which is the desktop's order, and a web address among them gets
its own web card and goes to the browser.

`deliveredOf` also only looked for `metadata` at the top level of the part. A
live part carries it there and a replayed one under `state`, so a conversation
opened from history showed nothing; it asks both now.
