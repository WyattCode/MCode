MCode Remote: the header says a turn is running

Claude replaces the clock with an amber ring while a turn is in flight -
239,181,68, 16dp across - and the ring is the pause button. The phone's header
stayed perfectly still during a reply, so the only sign of work was down at the
composer, a screen away from where the eye rests.

The ring is drawn beside the clock (the clock stays: Claude's own is still
there) and aborts the turn on a tap, the same request the composer's square
sends. Both values the header needs - whether a turn runs, and how to stop it -
are read once at the top of the header rather than at the leaf that uses them.

Build: gradle assembleDebug, installed on the pad (M367FC); the ring appeared
and turned during a live reply.
