MCode Remote: six marks under every reply, and a title that is the conversation's

Measured off Claude's transcript on this pad:

  each mark        34px of ink = 10.8dp
  centres          90px apart = 28.5dp
  the row          under every reply, not only the last one
  order            copy, share, read aloud, good, bad, retry

The phone had two marks (copy, more) on the final reply alone, which made the
controls look like they belonged to the end of the conversation rather than to
each answer. Copy and retry work; share, read and the two votes are drawn and
inert, because nothing in this app answers to them yet - the row's rhythm is
what is being matched, and a gap would leave it wrong.

`ThumbUp` and `ThumbDown` do not exist in Compose's icon set; the names are
`ThumbUpAlt` and `ThumbDownAlt`, which cost a build to find.

Two header corrections, both about a title that was not the conversation's:

  - With no name it said 「MCode」 - the app's own name where the title goes,
    which reads as a failed load. It says nothing now.

  - With no name it also showed only the folder underneath, so the header was a
    lone directory. Claude names a conversation after its first words and shows
    them at once; `headerTitle` does the same when the server has not named the
    conversation yet.
