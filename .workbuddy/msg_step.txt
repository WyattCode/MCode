MCode Remote: a step has a spine

Claude threads a rounded rule down the left of a step - the assistant's words
and the calls it made - 3.5dp wide, 216,216,214 on the pad, capped at both ends.
It is the only thing in the transcript that says those rows belong to one
another; without it a paragraph and the three calls under it read as four
unrelated lines.

Getting it to show took three corrections, each caught by looking at the screen:

- a Box beside the content measured zero height, because in a Row it sizes to
  its own content and a rule has none. It is a Canvas behind the content now.
- folding calls only forward left half the steps bare: the server sends the
  calls before the prose that describes them as often as after.
- and the fold itself attached a call to the nearest prose, which on the first
  row was the user's own prompt - a step must never take the reader's words.

Build: gradle assembleDebug, installed on the pad (M367FC); the rule draws beside
a prose-plus-call step at x=930-938, matching Claude's own.
