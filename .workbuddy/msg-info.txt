MCode Remote: the conversation's own record, as a card

`InfoPanel.vue` and `InfoSection.vue`: a card that says what the conversation
produced and what it read, so "which files did this touch" no longer means
scrolling the whole transcript.

Two sections are drawn, not four. 产物 and 来源 answer from the transcript
itself - what the tools wrote and what present_files handed over, less
everything produced, which is the desktop's rule that a file that came out of
a turn is not also a source for it. 环境信息 needs `git.summary` and a commit
flow the phone has no screen for, and 任务清单 needs a task registry the
gateway does not publish; a section that always read "0" would be worse than
no section, so they are left out rather than faked.

One thing measured and fixed while checking it: 来源 first listed 「交接」 and
「*(HANDOVER,handover,HANDOFF,ha...」 as files. Those are a grep call's
`pattern`, and taking a pattern for a path is nonsense. Only `read` and `list`
name a file, so only those count now - 51 entries became 41 real ones.

Verified on the pad: the card opens from the header's overflow (the wide
layout keeps its actions there), 产物 lists 3, 来源 lists 41, and both fold.
