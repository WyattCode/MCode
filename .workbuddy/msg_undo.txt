MCode Remote: undo and redo reach the phone

Both exist on the desktop - undo takes a turn's file edits back, redo puts them
again - and neither existed here, so a turn that broke a file could only be
repaired by hand from the phone.

They live in the header's overflow beside 重命名, in the same words the desktop
uses. Two items rather than a single toggle: undo acts on the last turn, redo on
the undo, and which one is available depends on where the conversation stands,
so naming them is clearer than guessing from a state flag.

Build: gradle assembleDebug, installed on the pad (M367FC); the menu shows both
items and 已插话 · 会尽快处理 now sits under a steered bubble.
