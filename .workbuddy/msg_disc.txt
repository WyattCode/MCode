MCode Remote: the disc steps aside for an approval

The way-back disc floats over the transcript at the pane's foot, so a pending
permission card landed underneath it: on the pad the disc sat across the second
card's 「拒绝并说明」 button, and a tap meant to scroll was very close to a tap
that answers the model.

The disc is hidden while any permission card is on screen - a queue is not a
place to invite scrolling - and returns as soon as the last one is answered.

Build: gradle assembleDebug, installed on the pad (M367FC); with two cards
queued the buttons are unobstructed and 「拒绝并说明」 opens its field cleanly.
