MCode Remote: a tool call is words, not a picture

Claude's tool row is a phrase and a chevron - the shell's name, or what is
being read - at 13sp of muted type and 53px of line height on the pad. This
put a 15dp glyph in front of every call, which built a column of little
pictures down the transcript that the original never had and pushed the words
to the right of where they are read from.

The chevron is now the same arrow the folded run uses, so a single call and a
run of them read alike.

Build: gradle assembleDebug, installed on the pad (M367FC).
