MCode Remote: the wave that parts two sittings

Claude marks a break in a conversation with a wavy rule and a clock, broken in
the middle for the time, rather than a straight line: 9px tall at 400dpi,
183,183,181, undulating at a 9dp pitch. A straight rule reads as a document
heading; the wave reads as time passing, which is what it is there for.

The phone now draws one wherever a reader comes back to a conversation after
more than five minutes, taken from each message's own `time.created`. A shorter
pause needs no mark.

Build: gradle assembleDebug, installed on the pad (M367FC); the divider showed
23:49 between the two sittings of the test thread.
