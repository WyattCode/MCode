MCode Remote: the branch chip switches, not just reports

`git.status` gives a branch name and `git.branches` gives the list, and the
desktop's home screen lets you switch from the chip. The phone printed the name
and stopped there, which is worse than not printing it: it looked like a
control and was not one.

The name in the change sheet is now a control - a chevron, then the branches
under it with the current one marked - and a tap sends `git.checkout` and takes
the new name.

Build: gradle assembleDebug, installed on the pad (M367FC).
