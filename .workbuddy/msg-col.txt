MCode Remote: the transcript's column, and four small lies in the list

The transcript was a third narrower than Claude's. Measured off its rendered
text nodes on this pad:

  first word starts  40px in from the pane  = 12.7dp   (was 28)
  line of body text  64px                   = 20.3dp
  body column        2368px wide            (was 2285)

28dp either side cost 100px of line length on a 1368-wide pane, which is why
our paragraphs wrapped earlier than the reference's and every reply ran longer
down the screen.

Three smaller corrections, each a line that said something untrue:

  - 「没有匹配的会话」 was drawn for an empty list as well as an empty search. The
    field is always on now, so that is the first thing a new install shows - it
    has to say 「还没有对话」 when there is no filter to blame.

  - The send disc carried `contentDescription = "语音输入"` in every state, so a
    screen reader found two microphones side by side. It reads 停止 while a turn
    runs and 语音输入 when the box is empty, which is what the disc does.

  - The list glyph has been a closed rectangle under a stroke since it was
    written (`M5 5h14v14H5z`), which renders as an empty box - every row showed
    one. It is a `<>` now, and a speech bubble for a conversation outside a
    folder.

Bubble geometry is still not measured. Claude's app on this pad opens onto its
Code remote-control screen rather than a chat transcript, so there is nothing
to sample below the fold; the numbers above came from its text nodes, which are
real, but radii and fills are still on the desktop stylesheet.
